                                                          ORDER
Challenge has been made to the order of the Tamil Nadu State Consumer Disputes Redressal
Commission, Chennai allowing the application filed by the respondent to examine the post-mortem
doctor to substantiate his case.

2. The respondent filed a complaint before the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission,
Tiruvannamalai for claiming compensation on the ground of medical negligence. Though earlier, he
has filed an application seeking to examine the experts that application has been dismissed by the
forum in CMP.No.66 of 2023. Thereafter, the same is confirmed by the State Forum. Subsequently,
another application has been filed by the respondent to examine the post-mortem doctor to prove
his case. That application was dismissed by the District Forum, on appeal, the application was

allowed by the State Consumer Forum. Challenging the same, the present revision is filed by the

3. The learned counsel for the petitioner submitted that since the District Consumer Forum and
State Consumer Forum had already dismissed the application to examine the experts, once again,
post mortem doctor cannot be examined by the respondent. Therefore, the order of the State
Consumer Forum has to be set aside.
4. Heard the learned counsel for the petitioner and perused the materials placed on record.

mortem. I have also gone through the post mortem certificate. The post mortem certificate would
indicate that the deceased had died of Pulmonary edema due to cardiac pathology aggravated by
surgical procedures. This Court is of the view that even if the doctor who post-mortem is examined,
he can confine his evidence only with respect to the post mortem alone and cannot substitute the
view of experts. Such view of the matter, I do not find any merits in this revision.
6. Accordingly, this revision stands dismissed. No costs. Consequently, connected miscellaneous
petition stands closed.
