1. The petitioners appear to have filed certain consumer complaints before National Consumer
Disputes Redressal Commission (NCDRC), alleging medical negligence against Saraswathy Hospital
and treating doctors.
2. It is in the case of the petitioners that the petitioner no. 2, underwent surgery on 21.10.2017,
which allegedly resulted in serious complications and 80% permanent disability.
3. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that though, there was some delay in compliance with
the directions passed by the NCDRC, however, the same was bona fide and has occurred
unintentionally.
4. It is stated that the petitioners do not have any sufficient means to earn their livelihood and are
unable to make the payment of the cost imposed vide orders dated 10.12.2024 and 02.05.2025.

5. Under these circumstances, the Court grants the petitioners liberty to point out the aforesaid

6. The submission of learned counsel for the petitioners is also placed on record that petitioners
shall fully cooperate with the Forum and shall not take any undue adjournments.
7. With the aforesaid observation, the petition stands disposed of.
