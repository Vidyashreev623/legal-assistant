Today, this matter has been listed under the caption 'for maintainability”.
2. The writ petition has been instituted to quash the order dated 13.06.2022 passed in C.C.No.18 of
2018, on the file of the District Consumer Redressal Forum at Tiruvallur.
3. The order under challenge is appealable to State Consumer Redressal Forum under the provisions
of the Consumer Protection Act. Thus, the petitioner is at liberty to prefer an appeal before the State
Consumer Redressal Commission Forum.
4. The 'Objection Note' raised by the Registry, High Court of Madras is in consonance with the
provisions of the Consumer Protection Act and the said Note has been approved. Consequently, the
Writ Petition in W.P.SR.No.71400 of 2022 is dismissed. The Registry is directed to return the
https://www.mhc.tn.gov.in/judis original copy of the impugned order to the learned Counsel on
record. No costs.
