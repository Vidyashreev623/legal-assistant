      A complaint came to be filed before the District Consumer Disputes
Redressal Commission, Kadapa District, alleging medical negligence by the
petitioners herein. The said District Forum, by virtue of an order dated
12.01.2024, partly allowed the complaint and awarded an amount of
Rs.4,08,921/- along with interest @ 6% per annum from the date of filing of
the complaint till the date of realization as compensation in favour of the
complainant - respondent No.5 herein.

      6.    On a perusal of the contents of the application as reproduced
hereinabove, it is clear that the appellants therein did not either by necessary
implication or explicitly state that they were unaware of the passing of the
order by District Consumer Forum. It is also clear that the petitioners had not
at all reflected the date of actual knowledge of the disposal of the case by the
District Consumer Forum and referred only to the date when the certified copy
had been issued i.e. on 31.08.2024. As to when the counsel had applied for
the certified copy of the order dated 12.01.2024, was also not reflected in the
application. The averments thus made in the application were quite vague and
general in nature.
      7.    It is in the backdrop of the aforementioned facts that the State
Consumer Commission, while placing reliance upon the Apex Court
judgments, among others, in the cases of Ram Lal and Ors. Vs. Rewa

         8.       Having gone through the relevant facts of the present case, as
also the law on the subject, in our opinion, the view expressed by the State
Commission, which was upheld by the National Consumer Commission,
cannot be interfered with, as the averments made in the application were
vague, in the absence whereof, no sufficient cause can be seen to have been
made, which would have warranted the condonation of delay.
         9.       Be that as it may, the writ petition is found to be without any merit
and is accordingly dismissed.
