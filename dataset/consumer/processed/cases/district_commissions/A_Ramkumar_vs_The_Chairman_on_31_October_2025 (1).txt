                                                     ORDER
This writ petition has been filed, challenging the impugned order dated 30.04.2024 passed by the
1st respondent and to direct the 1st respondent to take appropriate action against the 2nd
respondent for Medical Negligence as per law.
2. The case of the petitioner is that the petitioner's mother was admitted in the 2nd respondent's
Hospital for UTI and ultimately, she was turned into a coma and now vegetative state due to the
medical negligence of the 2nd respondent. Therefore, the petitioner made a complaint as against the
2nd respondent before the 1st respondent/Tamil Nadu Medical Council. However, the 1st
respondent closed the complaint stating no medical negligence on the part of the 2nd respondent.
Challenging the same, the present writ petition is filed.
https://www.mhc.tn.gov.in/judis ( Uploaded on: 25/11/2025 06:18:28 pm )
3. However, the learned counsel for the petitioner would submit that for the very same medical
negligence, the petitioner had approached the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission,
Chengalpattu, seeking compensation and the said Commission passed an order in favour of the
petitioner, awarding a sum of Rs.50,00,000/- as damages towards monetary loss and mental agony
suffered by the petitioner. The petitioner has already filed an appeal before the State Commission
seeking enhancement of compensation. Similarly, the second respondent has filed an appeal
challenging the order of the District Consumer Forum before the State Commission. Therefore, this
Court may close the writ petition, observing that the State Commission shall decide the appeals
independently, without being influenced by the observations made in the present impugned order.
4. The learned counsel appearing for the respondents have no objection to the submission made by
the learned counsel for the petitioner. https://www.mhc.tn.gov.in/judis ( Uploaded on: 25/11/2025
06:18:28 pm )
5. In view of the above, the Tamil Nadu State Consumer Redressal Commission, Chennai, is
required to decide the issue raised in the appeal independently, without being influenced by the
impugned order, and to pass appropriate orders. Accordingly, this writ petition stands disposed of.
No costs.
