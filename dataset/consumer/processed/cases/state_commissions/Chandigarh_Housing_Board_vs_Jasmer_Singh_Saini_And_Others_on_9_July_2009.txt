 O R D E R 
ASHOK BHAN J., PRESIDENT   Chandigarh Housing Board-petitioner herein, which was the
Opposite Party No.1 before the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Forum-I, Union Territory,
Chandigarh (hereinafter referred to as the District Forum for short) has filed the present Revision
Petition.
Briefly stated, the facts of the case are:-
 
Chandigarh Housing Board-petitioner herein and the Administrator, Union
Territory, Chandigarh floated a scheme known as The Chandigarh Allotment of Sites
to Co-operative Societies Scheme, 1979. In the said Scheme, the people who were
aspiring for residential units in the city were invited to form house building
co-operative societies duly registered under the Punjab Co-operative Societies Act,
1961. A few promoters of Co-operative Societies under the aforesaid Scheme floated a
house building society known as The Chandigarh Sector 18 Co-operative House
Building Society Limited. Respondent no.1/complainant applied for membership and
was, accordingly, allotted membership no. 93 on 22.04.1982. He deposited total
amount of Rs.36,300/- on account of earnest money as demanded by respondent
no.3, from time to time, which was duly transferred to the bank account of the
petitioner through the Society. Petitioner and the Administrator, Union Territory of
Chandigarh unduly delayed the allotment of land to the Society and demanded
additional amount from the Society. Due to change in the family circumstances and
job placement, the complainant requested the Society/respondent no. 3 to refund the
amount deposited by him, i.e., Rs.36,300/- with interest vide his Application dated
17.07.1997. The President of the Society directed the complainant to make an
Application with the petitioner as well. Accordingly, the complainant filed another
application on 27.07.1997 with the petitioner and respondent no.2. By its letter dated
13.08.1997, petitioner directed the complainant to submit a demand for refund of the
amount through the Society. Consequently, the complainant filed an Application on
18.09.1997 to the Society requesting for the refund of the amount. After several
correspondences, he received a letter dated 10.03.2000 from the petitioner through
the Society/respondent no. 3 wherein an amount of Rs.32,670/- was refunded to him
after deducting 10% of the total earnest money, i.e., Rs.3,630/- which was accepted
by the complainant vide its letter dated 07.04.2000 under protest. No interest was
given, although the amount paid by the complainant had been deposited in the bank.

Complainant filed a complaint before the District Forum with a prayer that the petitioner be
directed to refund the sum of Rs.3,630/- deducted by it while refunding the amount with interest @
18%. Compensation of Rs.5,000/- was also demanded.
 
Respondent nos. 2 and 3 did not appear in spite of service of Notice. Only petitioner put in
appearance and contested the case. It was stated that the amount had been refunded to the
petitioner as per the prevalent instructions issued by the Chandigarh Administration. The District
Forum, after taking into consideration, the material on record, allowed the complaint and directed
the petitioner to refund the deducted amount of Rs.3,630/- with interest @ 10% p.a. from the date
of deduction till realisation. Interest @ 10% p.a. on the amount of Rs.32,670/- from the date of
deposit till refund, i.e., 10.03.2000, was also awarded.
 
Aggrieved against the Order of the District Forum, petitioner filed an Appeal before the State
Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission, Chandigarh (hereinafter referred to as the State
Commission for short), which has been dismissed by the impugned Order. It was held that the
petitioner was not entitled to deduct 10% of the earnest money as per the fresh instructions issued
by the Union Territory Administration, Chandigarh on 10.03.2000. That as per instructions dated
10.03.2000, petitioner was also liable to pay interest @ 10% as petitioner was not empowered to
deduct 10% of the earnest money.
 
Counsel for the parties have been heard at length.
 
Petitioner had deducted 10% of the earnest money deposited by the respondent/complainant under
old instructions. Under old instructions, no interest was payable on the refund amount. On
10.03.2000, the Finance Secretary, Chandigarh Administration, issued fresh instructions, relevant
Clauses of which reads as under:-
   
2. Simple interest @ 10% per annum on the money already deposited by Societies
shall be paid by the Chandigarh Housing Board by adjustment through book transfer.
Interest shall be calculated from the date of each deposit till the date the difference in
25% of the earnest money xxxx (sic) revised rates is deposited.

7. Full refund of earnest money will henceforth be made to the Societies/and its members without
forfeiting 10% of the 25% earnest money deposited.
     
Relying upon these instructions, it has been held by the State Commission that the petitioner was
entitled to refund the entire earnest money without forfeiting 10% of the earnest money deposited
and the complainant was entitled to interest @ 10% on the amount deposited.
 
Question that arises for consideration is whether the instructions dated 10.03.2000 issued by the
Union Territory Administration, Chandigarh would be applicable to the case in hand or whether the
case would be governed by the earlier instructions or the prevalent practice of the Union Territory
Administration, Chandigarh at the relevant time? The complainants/respondents case was
processed under the old instructions by which the Housing Board could deduct 10% of the earnest
money deposited, in case the amount was to be refunded at the request of the claimant and no
interest was payable on the same. The case of the respondent/complainant was processed under the
old instructions which is clear from letter dated 10.03.2000 vide which cheque no. 231840 dated
25.02.2000 for a sum of Rs.32,670/- was sent to the respondent/complainant towards the refund of
the earnest money deposited by him. The same reads as under:-
   
Please refer to your letter dated 29.9.1999 on the subject noted above. Find enclosed
herewith cheque No. 231840 dated 25.2.2000 for Rs.32,670/- (Rupees Thirty Two
Thousand Six Hundred and Seventy only) drawn on Chandigarh State Co-op. Bank
Ltd., Sector 22-B, Chandigarh in your favour. This amount represent the refund of
E.M. of following members after deducting 10% of 25% amount. The name of this
member from the record available with the Board has been deleted.
 
Sl.No. Name of Amount Amt. Amt.
Member deposited forfeited to be With the by the refunded Board Board equal to 10%
of 25%
--------------------------------------------------------------
1. Sh. J.S. Saini 36,300/- 3,630/- 32,670/-.

This letter clearly shows that the decision to refund the amount had been taken much prior to the
issuance of the instructions. It further shows that the cheque had been prepared on 25.02.2000.
Simply, because the cheque was forwarded to the complainant on 10.03.2000, would not mean that
the instructions dated 10.03.2000 would be applicable. These instructions directed the petitioner
to:-
   
take further action on all the pending cases in the light of above decisions     which
shows that the instructions were prospective in nature and were not clarificatory or
retrospective. The instructions were to apply on pending cases but the same were not
to be applied to the cases where the decision had already been taken. Complainant
had applied for refund of the money in the year 1997, much prior to the issuance of
the instructions dated 10.03.2000. His case had been processed earlier to the
issuance of the instructions dated 10.03.2000. His case, under the circumstances,
had to be governed under old instructions.
   
The State Commission clearly erred in coming to the conclusion that the instructions
issued on 10.03.2000 would be applicable as the amount was refunded to the
respondent/complainant vide letter dated 10.03.2000. In our opinion, the
instructions dated 10.03.2000 could not be applied to the cases in which the decision
of the refund had already been taken. It was to be applied to the cases wherein the
decision to refund the amount has yet not been taken.
   
Accordingly, while setting aside the Order passed by the Fora below, we hold that the
instructions of 10.03.2000 are not applicable to the case of the complainant. But, in
equity, we feel that the petitioner was not justified in the delay caused in processing
of the refund of the deposited amount for nearly 2 years. Accordingly, we direct the
petitioner to pay interest @ 12% p.a. on the refunded amount of Rs.32,670/- with
effect from the date the complainant applied for refund, i.e., 17.07.1997 upto the date
of dispatch of cheque, i.e., 10.03.2000 for delayed refund and payment. Dismissed.
No costs.
